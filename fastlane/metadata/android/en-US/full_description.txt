<p><b>CashBandit</b> is a simple, colorful cash book for your phone. All data stays on the device: <b>100 % offline</b>, no internet permission, no account, no ads, no trackers. The app is in German.</p>

<p><b>Features</b></p>
<ul>
<li>Record income and expenses with amount, date, category and note</li>
<li>Categories with their own color and icon – fully editable, reorder by drag &amp; drop</li>
<li>Weekly or monthly overview: balance, stacked bar charts by category, comparison of the last 12 months</li>
<li>Breakdown by category with percentage</li>
<li>Booking list grouped by day, filter by period, category, type and search in notes</li>
<li>Recurring bookings (weekly to yearly), filled in automatically when the app is opened</li>
<li>App lock with PIN and fingerprint, content hidden in the app switcher</li>
<li>Backup as JSON (export and import) and export of bookings as CSV</li>
<li>Light and dark mode</li>
</ul>
